Terms of use

Who publishes Who thinks of it? and the simple rules for using it.

The service

Who thinks of it? (quiypense.org) lets a couple or a household take stock of how the household tasks are shared: who does them, and who thinks of them. It is free, with no sign-up and no adverts. It can also be used from assistants (Claude, ChatGPT…) through its MCP server.

What the result is, and what it is not

The result is based on your answers and on average durations, taken from the Time Use Survey by INSEE (the French national statistics institute), which you can adjust. It is a starting point for talking things over, not an exact measurement, nor a diagnosis, nor medical, psychological or legal advice. The site does not judge and does not point the finger at anyone.

How you use it

The service is meant for personal use. The sharing link contains your answers and your first names: you choose who you send it to. Please do not use the site in a way that disrupts it (automated submissions, attempts to get around the anti-robot protection).
